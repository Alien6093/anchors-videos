## 1. Concept, tone, emotional arc

A silent, exact, confident film in the register of a great Linear or Stripe product film. The chat is the whole world; one column, nothing but the conversation and the widgets CLEO drops into it. Captions are dry, at most 8 words, never repeat what the UI already shows, and sit in the side or bottom margin so they never cover a reading target. Every scene has ONE thing to read; the rest of the frame is dimmed to about 35%. Orange is reserved for actions and final moments (Approve, Set live date, pay, live, final numbers); selections and highlights use a neutral cream highlight.

**Arc for 150s:**
- **Hook (0-4.5):** the finished result for 1.6s, black, then the promise.
- **Curiosity (4.5-23):** a website, real choices, the plan.
- **Control (23-70):** creators, one credit, the cut to 8 and the budget cap, briefs written with care, the quote.
- **Commitment (70-104.5):** pay, briefs sent, drafts, review against the brief, Approve is final, three go back with exact words. Near-silence 103.3-104.5.
- **Release (104.5-120):** 8 of 8, dates set, live.
- **Proof (120-145.5):** "Two weeks later." 2,80,000 impressions as the crest, then two quick supports (comments, audience).
- **End (145.5-150):** hold.

Look: claude.ai dark chat, serif Claude replies, grey collapsed tool lines, cream "anchors / CLEO" widgets. The dashboard page is the only light full-bleed screen. Camera grammar: assign one move per scene (push-in, lateral pan, pull-back, macro punch-in to 130-140% on one number, rack-focus, whip) and never repeat a move in adjacent scenes.
