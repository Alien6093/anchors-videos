## 1. Data-view decision (3 lines) and concept

**Decision: both, in a fixed order: a Day-three snapshot, then the fixed finals.**
1. A live campaign's real question is "is it on track?", so the film opens on a mid-campaign snapshot (Fri 9 Oct 2026, 8:00 PM, all 8 posted, Priyanshu and Darika under 11 hours old): numbers still climbing, a top four that will change. That is what makes it LIVE metrics rather than a report.
2. Then "Two weeks later." and the number climbs from 1,85,700 to 2,80,000 as the crest, so the fixed finals (v4 section 7) land unchanged and the plan-vs-actual story (Rs 525 vs Rs 540, above the 2.69-2.77 lakh forecast) has a start and an end.
3. Cost: 3 scenes (11.8s; 15.0s with the context scene 2) of new numbers, all derived arithmetically from the finals (section 6), flagged NEW in this script only (no on-screen label, as v4); everything from scene 7 on is verbatim v4 figures (scene 10 uses Ashish's final row: 44,800 impressions, 728 likes, 63 comments, 1.77%). The small texture lines in scene 10 ("Last synced Fri 23 Oct 2026", "Published on Wed 7 Oct 2026, 10:00 AM", "Content changed after approval: No") are dramatised and flagged for the product check (section 7 item 11).

**Tone:** silent, exact, confident; captions dry, at most 7 words, never repeat what the UI shows. Orange is reserved for the final numbers (scene 7 CPM tile, scene 8 totals lock) and the CTA. The snapshot uses neutral cream highlights so the finals feel earned.

**Arc for 60s:** Hook (0-4.3) the answer flashes, then the promise. Context (4.3-7.5) 8 Live. Snapshot (7.5-19.3) numbers arriving. Breath (19.3-21.4). Crest (21.4-30.0) the count-up. Proof (30.0-55.7) table, plan vs actual, how one creator's post performed (38.6-42.9), comments (42.9-49.3), campaign audience (49.3-55.7). End (55.7-60.0).
