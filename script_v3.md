## 1. Concept, tone, emotional arc

A silent, exact, confident film in the register of a great Linear or Stripe product film. The chat is the whole world; one column, no sidebar; nothing but the conversation and the widgets CLEO drops into it. Captions are dry and short and never explain what the UI already shows. Orange is reserved for actions and the moments something becomes final (Approve, Set live date, payment, live).

**Arc for 150s:**
- **Curiosity (0-14):** a website, one sentence, a chat that starts asking smart questions.
- **Control (14-77):** plan, 16 creators, deep dive (1 credit), the cut to 8, briefs written with care. The viewer sees the brand steering, not just clicking.
- **Commitment (77-111):** pay, drafts, review against the brief, Approve is irreversible, three go back with exact words. Music thins to near silence at 109.5-111.0.
- **Release (111-125):** 8 of 8, dates set, live. Big drop, chime layers, cymbal swell.
- **Proof (125-150):** 2,80,000 reached, sentiment, audience, end card.

Look: claude.ai dark (#262624-ish), serif Claude replies, grey collapsed tool lines, native dark tables; cream "anchors / CLEO" widgets; Claude-orange accent. Caption cards: white, heavy grotesk, lower-left or center, "anchors" lowercase.
