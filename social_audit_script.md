## 2. STAGE 1 audit (Build)

**Hook, first 1s:** an orange "LIVE." slab over a dim, blurred table. No question, no promise, no subject. Cover frame (0s) is a dark blurred board with one word: not a thumbnail that sells (4:5 cover is better: a real sentence).
**First 3s:** LIVE / PAID / 16 to 8 are three outcomes in reverse chronological order, none explained. A stranger gets "something is live, something was paid, 16 became 8" and still does not know what the product does. Title "One chat. Whole campaign." lands at 1.7s but names no object (campaign of what?). The word "creator" never appears in a 9:16 caption (only in the "8 creators" chips and the pill "03 CREATORS"); the 4:5 hook does say it. LinkedIn is not named until the 40s "Live on LinkedIn." and Claude only on the end card.
**Arc:** setup (website), no tension (every step succeeds), payoff = "Pay once." The emotional peak is a payment confirmation, then the film continues past the payoff into dates and live: the arc has two endings. CTA arrives after a 2s dead tease.
**Stranger test (muted, 50s):** understands "some AI chat makes a plan and a payment." Does not learn who it is for (brands? agencies? HR-tech?), what a creator campaign is, what Claude has to do with it, or why it beats the current way. FAIL.

## 5. CROSS-CUTTING STORY PROBLEMS (series level)

1. **Not a series.** No "Part 1/2/3" chip, no consistent series title, teasers only on Parts 1 and 2 and they point to the wrong next video (Part 2 says "set live dates"; Part 3 starts on results). Only the Stage 1 LinkedIn post says "part 1 of 3".
2. **Timeline loop.** Part 1 goes to LIVE; Part 2 rewinds to briefs and ends "Next: set live dates"; Part 3 opens with posts already live. A viewer who sees Part 1 first thinks Part 2 is a replay.
3. **Hooks sell outcomes without a subject** (Parts 1 and 3) or answer themselves (Part 2). Only Part 3's frame 0 is a thumbnail.
4. **Nobody says who it is for or why.** Across about 150s of captions the benefit is never stated. "Run your creator campaign in a chat." appears only on the end card, after the viewer has decided.
5. **Feature-label captions** ("Sort them your way", "Cost and forecast follow", "Format. Link. Files.", "Ranks moved") and caption-repeats-UI redundancy (the Stage 1 `Cost and forecast follow` and Stage 3 CPM x5).
6. **Three CTA lines** (Connect anchors to Claude / Brief your creators in Claude / Ask Claude: How is it performing?) with one typed URL; no follow, comment, save, or "link in bio". On Shorts/Reels a typed URL in a video frame is not an action. The tagline small print "Zeko AI, live on LinkedIn." is on Parts 1 and 3 only.
7. **Integrity/disclosure** (section X): scripted metrics presented as a real campaign; labels removed in round 3, re-added only in Part 2 and invisible; named creators and commenters; ranking real people; 8 removed creators named.
8. **Indian number/format friction** (lakh, Rs, 2,80,000, CPM, "Effective CPM") for global Shorts/LinkedIn viewers with no gloss.
9. **Posting copy conflicts with the video** (IG "live in three days", S2 IG opens with an old title, voice switches between "We ran", "We sent", "I asked").
10. **Reading load.** Caption word counts are fine (1-1.5 w/s). The load is UI text + callout chip + caption landing in the same 2s (Stage 1: 25-27s and 36-39s; Stage 3: 17-18s about 7 w/s).
